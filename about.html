Our Foundation

5+ Years of Hands-On Industry Experience

A clear distinction between personal leadership experience and corporate transparency.

STN Trucking Ltd. operates with a singular mission: to make freight transportation, residential relocations, and property hauling transparent, predictable, and stress-free for customers across Surrey, Vancouver, the Lower Mainland, and connecting British Columbia corridors.

Managing Director Abbas brings over five years of dedicated field experience across Canadian commercial trucking, residential moving, and property service dispatch. Having managed long-haul runs throughout British Columbia as well as high-density urban relocations, Abbas founded STN Trucking Ltd. to eliminate the common headaches of the transportation industry: hidden fees, unreturned calls, and careless property damage.

Whether transporting a delicate heirloom sofa across Surrey, moving a multi-bedroom family house in Vancouver, clearing renovation debris, or dispatching a 53-ft dry van across British Columbia highways, our team upholds the highest standard of cargo strapping, protective blanketing, and respectful customer service.

Corporate Transparency Commitment: We never fabricate founding years, artificial awards, or inflated review statistics. The 5+ years claim reflects the verified industry experience of Abbas, Managing Director. All customer reviews are authentic and subject to dispatch verification.
